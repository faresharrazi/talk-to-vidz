**Situation:** Your webinar on Livestorm has just concluded and you want an email template to leverage the event content to provide value to your wider audience (mainly marketing leads) and potentially attract them to future events in an email. This mail is directed to contacts who were not supposed to attend the event.

**Objective:** Create a single follow-up email

**Instruction:**
1. Analyze the transcript for valid content. Valid content includes meaningful sentences, keywords related to the webinar topic, and a reasonable length. If the transcript consists only of empty strings, whitespace, or irrelevant filler (e.g., 'um,' 'ah,' 'thank you'), consider it empty.
2. If the transcript is empty, output the message: 'Transcript is empty.'
3. If not empty, Analyze the transcript to identify:
   a. The host's persona (example: promoter penny, trainer tracy, seller sammy, educator emily, facilitator franky)
   b. The event type (example: webinar, training session, product demo, internal all-hands)
   c. The main topic and objectives of the event
   d. Detect the language and generate content EXCLUSIVELY in that language
4. Based on the identified persona, use the corresponding suggested content below to structure your email. Fill in each section with relevant information from the transcript.
5. Rely strictly on the provided transcript, without including external information.
6. CRITICAL: Generate content EXCLUSIVELY in the detected language of the transcript
7. DO NOT translate, provide multiple languages, or mix languages
8. If the transcript is in Spanish, generate ONLY Spanish emails
9. If the transcript is in English, generate ONLY English emails
10. If the transcript is in French, generate ONLY French emails
11. And so on for any other language

**Suggested content for each persona:**

**For Promoter Penny - Email Newsletter for Contact Nurturing**
*A marketing professional focused on generating interest and leads through engaging content and events. Typical use cases include marketing webinars, roundtables, expert testimonials.*

Content:
Event summary:
- A concise 2-3 paragraph overview of the webinar's main topic and key points
- This provides quick value for those who didn't attend and want to learn more about the content
Key takeaways:
- 3-5 bullet points highlighting the most important insights from the webinar
- These should be actionable and provide immediate value to readers
Speaker quotes:
- 2-3 impactful quotes from the speaker(s), highlighting their expertise and the webinar's value
- These can be used to create social media-friendly content and encourage sharing
Q&A highlights:
- A selection of 2-3 interesting questions from the audience and their answers
- This demonstrates the interactive nature of our webinars and the depth of insights provided
Downloadable resource (if available):
- A practical, value-added item related to the webinar topic such as a checklist, template, or mini-guide
- This gives recipients something tangible to implement and keeps our brand top-of-mind
Full webinar recording:
- Access to the complete webinar recording for those who want to watch or rewatch
- This allows us to capture leads who might be interested in the full content
Next event teaser:
- A brief description of our upcoming webinar or event, including the topic, date, and main value proposition
- This helps build interest and anticipation for future events
Feedback form link:
- A short survey to gather opinions on the webinar content and suggestions for future topics
- This helps us improve and shows we value attendee input

**For Educator Emily - Follow-up Recap Email of the Class**
*A teacher or instructor who shares knowledge with others through online courses and educational webinars. Typical use cases include online courses and education.*

Content:
- Summary of key concepts covered
- Important formulas, definitions, or principles
- Links to recommended reading or study materials
- Practice problems or exercises
- Preview of upcoming topics in the next class
- Reminders of upcoming assignments or exams
- Study tips or strategies related to the material
- Office hours or support resources available
- Encouragement or motivational message
- Any changes to the syllabus or course schedule

**Input Data:** Livestorm event transcript

**Output format for all suggest content:**

- Format: Markdown
- Generate content EXCLUSIVELY in the detected language of the transcript
- DO NOT translate or provide multiple language versions
- Never mention the persona
- Write in a natural email format with flowing paragraphs
- No bullet points or lists
- Do not use any square brackets, placeholders, or variables - use specific details from the transcript only
- Subject line should be written as plain text, for example:
  "Missed our webinar? Here are the key insights from today's session"
  "Watch the replay: Complete guide to digital marketing strategies"
- Avoid formal sections or labels
- Create natural paragraph transitions
- Write as if having a genuine conversation with the recipient
- Include specific examples and details from the actual event
- 200-300 words
- Provide 1 natural subject line option
- Maintain a warm, conversational tone throughout
- Avoid subtitles in the content and only use paragraphs

**Additional notes:**

- Each paragraph should flow naturally into the next
- Include specific numbers, quotes, and examples from the transcript
- Avoid generic phrases like "key takeaways" or "main points"
- Write as if recapping the event to a colleague over coffee
